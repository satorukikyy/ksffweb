# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js (user's choice, 2026-09-24). "Rapih, cakep, bucin, modern."

## Users

One reader: the owner's long-distance partner, opening a private link on their phone, usually at the edges of the day (just woke up, can't sleep, missing the other person). The owner edits the content.

## Product Purpose

A private love site for a long-distance couple, written in English. It shortens the distance a little: shows both local times, how long they've been together, how long until the next visit, and keeps letters and small notes the partner can open when they need them. Success: the partner smiles, comes back to it on hard days, and it never feels cringe.

## Positioning

Made for two specific people in two specific cities. Everything personal (names, cities, dates, memories, letters) comes from the owner, not from a template.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- English copy.
- Affectionate but not over the top ("ga over"): cute, polished, modern.
- Inferred (user skipped the question round, confirm later): voice is first person, from the owner to the partner; all four areas wanted ("lengkap"): time zones + countdown, open-when letters, memories timeline, small playful bits.
- Private page: should not be indexed by search engines.
- Owner-editable in lib/us.ts: letters, reasons, coupons, and `day` (what Kiky is probably doing at each WIB hour). Coupon and day lines are starters until the owner makes them true.

## Evidence on Hand

- Names: Kiky (owner, the guy) and Amanda Safira, "Amanda" (the reader).
- Cities: Amanda in Bireuen, Aceh; Kiky in Depok, West Java. Both WIB (Asia/Jakarta), so no time difference; distance and the ~40 min solar offset are the real LDR facts.
- Still missing, never invent: anniversary date, next visit date, photos, memories. Use clearly marked placeholders.

## Product Principles

1. Sweet, never syrupy: one warm line beats five hearts.
2. Personal over generic: every real detail comes from the owner.
3. Made for a phone in one hand at night.
4. Quiet delight: small moments of play, nothing that performs. The stick figures are the exception the owner asked for (2026-09-26, "makin lucu gemes"): openly cute, but always drawn by the same pen and always answering something Amanda did or the real clock.
